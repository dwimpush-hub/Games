          Click or tap a piece, then a gold square. Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Enter selects, C casts, Esc cancels, U undo, H hint, N new duel, G grimoire, F feats, M mute.

          Settings

          Sound and motion

          Off

          Voices come from your system. For better ones on a Mac: System Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › System Voice › Manage Voices, download an Enhanced or Premium English voice, then reload the game. Automatic picks the best one installed.

          Keys

          Arrows move the cursor, Enter selects, Escape cancels. Click a key to change it, then press the new one.

          Gamepad: d-pad or stick moves, A selects, B cancels, X casts, Y hints, Start opens the menu.

          Spell puzzles

          Each is mate in one, and only a spell will do it. Solve them to learn what every spell is for.

          1. Pick a puzzle below. The board loads with exactly enough Spell Power for its spell.
          2. Select the glowing piece, press Cast Spell, then move it to the purple square that gives mate. The sidebar counts the three steps with you.
          3. A wrong move puts the pieces back. Leave puzzle in the sidebar returns you to duelling.
