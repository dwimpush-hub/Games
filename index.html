Wizard Chess Order vs Chaos

OZ-ventures! Follow the Yellow Brick Road

A 3D fly-through board game. Pick Dorothy, the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman or the Cowardly Lion and race three AI travellers through six zones of Oz: roll, dodge the Witch's curses, ride a tornado, mend the road before the Winged Monkeys march, and answer the riddle at the Emerald Gates. Every card has its moment on the board, and each zone has its own music.
